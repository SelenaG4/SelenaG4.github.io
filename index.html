Case Study

TechVista Consultancy — Group 9

Documentation Strategy for a Real Client Engagement

Client: Ansen Innovation (Melbourne digital transformation firm) & its client, the Federation of Chinese Medicine & Acupuncture Societies of Australia (FCMA)  ·  University of Melbourne, Master of Information Systems  ·  Semester 2, 2024  ·  5-person team

Ansen Innovation had shipped five new features to its client FCMA's membership website but, under budget and deadline pressure, had no documentation and no long-term documentation strategy. TechVista Consultancy (my student consulting team, operating under the name for this engagement) was brought in to close both gaps: Component A — documentation support for the five live features — and Component B — a long-term documentation strategy for Ansen's internal use.

1Scoping & client governance

Coordinated client correspondence with Ansen's representative throughout the engagement. Built stakeholder maps and three detailed personas — an Ansen developer, an FCMA administrator, and an FCMA end-user practitioner — to ground requirements before any documentation work began, then set the delivery roadmap (Gantt chart and milestone plan) that kept both components on schedule.

2Methodology selection

Evaluated BPMN against the Unified Modelling Language (UML) for process documentation and selected UML for its versatility and closer fit to Ansen's software-development context. Used the APQC Process Classification Framework to calibrate the right level of process granularity — Level 4 activities on the main flowcharts, with Level 5 tasks folded in as supporting detail rather than cluttering the primary diagrams.

3Validation

Designed and ran two rounds of understandability testing, adapting a methodology from HCI/metamodel-notation research (Patig, 2008, as used by SAP to validate their own notations). Round 1 tested the UML notation itself in isolation and scored 95% correctness at a 21-second average response time; round 2 tested the five completed process models and informed further simplification before delivery.

4Deliverables

  • User Guide Pack — simplified, implementation-ready guides for FCMA's end users (practitioners and members).
  • Admin Guide Pack — process guides for FCMA's administrative staff.
  • Process Diagram Pack — UML as-is flowcharts for Ansen's development team.

Each pack was tailored in tone, branding, and depth to its specific audience — covering five new features: online membership application, membership renewal, event registration, CPD record management, and member detail updates.

5Team outcome — Component B

Alongside Component A, the wider team delivered Ansen's long-term documentation strategy: a POPIT gap analysis of the current state, a technology portfolio built around a Pace-Layered Architecture (a foundational Issue Tracking/Version Control layer, a workflow-automation enhancement layer, and an experimental layer for automated post-launch documentation), a cost breakdown, and data-governance/metadata guidelines — the strategic complement to Component A's tactical documentation packs.

This engagement was delivered as coursework for ISYS90119 (University of Melbourne) by a 5-person team operating as "TechVista Consultancy." The summary above focuses on my own contribution — project management/coordination and the Component A documentation/process- modeling workstream — within a shared team deliverable.
